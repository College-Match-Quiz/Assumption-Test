About our platform

Helping students find the right college fit with confidence.

Assumption-Test was built to make the college decision process clearer, smarter, and more personal. Instead of relying on generic rankings or polished marketing, we focus on what actually matters to students: academic goals, campus culture, learning environment, affordability, and long-term fit.

01

Purpose

We help students compare schools based on their real priorities rather than surface-level reputation.

02

Decision-making

Our matching approach considers academics, social environment, cost, setting, and student needs.

03

Outcome

The goal is simple: help students choose schools where they are more likely to thrive and succeed.

Our Mission

Our mission is to give students clarity in one of the biggest decisions of their lives. We use structured guidance and personalized matching to move beyond generic advice and toward recommendations that reflect each student’s goals, values, and realistic constraints.

What We Help With

  • Finding schools that align with academic interests and career goals
  • Comparing campus environment, class size, and social culture
  • Evaluating affordability, distance from home, and day-to-day practicality
  • Supporting transfer students with articulation and credit-transfer considerations

Why It Matters

A school should not just be impressive on paper. It should be a place where a student can grow academically, feel supported socially, and make informed decisions about their future. We aim to reduce stress and improve confidence during the college search.

What guides us

Transparency

We explain the reasoning behind recommendations so decisions feel informed, not random.

Personalization

Every student has different goals, and every recommendation should reflect that reality.

Accessibility

College planning should feel more approachable, not overwhelming or intimidating.
